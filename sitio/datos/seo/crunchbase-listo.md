## Full Description (la larga)

```
Sacs is a retail management platform built for one industry: fashion. Since 2014 it has served clothing stores, boutiques, shoe stores, jewelry stores, own-label brands and wholesalers across Mexico, from a single boutique to chains of dozens of locations.

What sets it apart is that the entire system is built around size and colour, which is how apparel and footwear are actually sold and controlled. Inventory, point of sale, online store, wholesale, purchasing, customers and WhatsApp run on one database, so there is nothing to sync between tools: when the last piece of a size sells on the floor, it goes out of stock online in the same second.

The platform covers the operation end to end: a point of sale that works with or without internet and sends the receipt over WhatsApp; inventory by size and colour across locations, with transfers and physical counts; an online store and marketplace channels on the same stock; B2B wholesale with price lists per customer; layaway and pre-orders; loyalty and customer records; purchasing and supplier orders; and reporting on sell-through and size curves, the numbers a fashion retailer actually decides with.

Sacs is used by more than 3,000 active brands in Mexico and Latin America and holds a 4.7 rating across 113 Google reviews. Pricing is public and charged per location: from 527 MXN per location per month on the annual plan, or 810 MXN monthly.

The company operates from Santiago de Querétaro, Mexico.
```
